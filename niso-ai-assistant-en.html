Who Is Niso?

Niso is an AI-powered assistant built by New Soul Advertising (New Soul Marketing Solutions) specifically to serve its clients — making it easier to get in touch and faster to move projects forward. Niso isn't just a chatbot; it's the first point of contact for clients, helping them find the right service, answering questions, and suggesting the best marketing solutions, any time.

Niso helps clients with:

  • Learning about New Soul's services.
  • Choosing the right service for their business.
  • Suggesting advertising and marketing ideas.
  • Getting ready for exhibitions and conferences.
  • Learning about booth design and build.
  • Directing clients to the fastest way to reach New Soul's team.
